Jewellery & gold scales

Made for the sarafa counter and the assay lab.

Our balances read in the units the jewellery trade actually uses. Density balances give gold results directly, so a sample is graded in two steps.

  • Trade units built inAK Series: g, TMR, PKT, ct, TAR, Tola, GSM, lb, oz, ozt, dwt, t and kg.
  • Gold, silver, palladium resultsMDJ density balances show density, purity, K number and volume for gold, and purity for silver, palladium and K gold.
  • Dual display for customer and counterFront and back screens on AK and EK Series show the same weight to both sides of the counter.
